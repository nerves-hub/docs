NervesHub turns the metrics and alarms your devices report into a health status per device, charts of each metric over time, and a list of the alarms currently raised. This page covers the NervesHub side: where the data shows up and how you set the thresholds that decide whether a device is healthy.

The data comes from the device. To send it, set up the [Health extension](/extensions/health) in your firmware and turn it on for the product.

## Where the data comes from

| What you see | Sent by |
| --- | --- |
| Metrics, including your own | The [Health extension](/extensions/health). Devices on a client that implements it can use the standalone [Metrics extension](/extensions/metrics-and-alarms). |
| Alarms | The [Health extension](/extensions/health). Devices on a client that implements it can use the standalone [Alarms extension](/extensions/metrics-and-alarms). |
| Health status | Worked out by NervesHub from the metrics and the product's health profile. |

<Note>
  `nerves_hub_link` 2.14.0 sends metrics and alarms inside the Health report. It does not implement the standalone Metrics or Alarms extensions. See [Metrics and Alarms extensions](/extensions/metrics-and-alarms) for which clients do.
</Note>

## Requirements

- The Health extension turned on for the product and the device. A product created in the web UI starts with every extension on. A product created through the API or `nh` starts with every extension off. See [Extensions overview](/extensions/overview).
- NervesHub 2.5.0 or later for health profiles.
- ClickHouse on the instance for metric history charts, alarm history, and health profiles. See [What works without ClickHouse](#what-works-without-clickhouse) and [Feature prerequisites](/hosting/feature-prerequisites#clickhouse).

## Health status

Each device has one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Healthy** | No metric in the device's health profile has reached a threshold. |
| **Warning** | At least one metric has reached its warning threshold. |
| **Unhealthy** | At least one metric has reached its unhealthy threshold. |
| **Unknown** | NervesHub has nothing to judge: the profile has no metrics, or the device has not reported any of them. |

NervesHub works the status out again each time the device reports metrics. When the device is not connected, its badge reads **No recent data**.

The status appears in these places:

- The **Health** card on the device's **Details** tab, with a tile for each featured metric.
- The **Now** card on the device's **Health** tab, with every metric the device last reported. The **Health** tab carries a dot while a threshold is engaged.
- The devices list. Filter by health under **Status**, and by alarm or metric value under **Alarms and metrics**. See [Finding devices](/management/finding-devices).

A change of status can also be sent to your own systems as the `device.health_status_changed` event. See [Webhooks](/outbound/webhooks).

## Health profiles

A health profile is the set of thresholds that decides a device's status. Profiles belong to a product.

Every product has a **Default** profile. You can add a profile for a platform, which applies to devices whose firmware reports that platform. A device uses its platform's profile if there is one, and **Default** otherwise.

### Default thresholds

A new product's **Default** profile starts with four metrics, each measured over 1 hour and each featured:

| Metric | Warning at | Unhealthy at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cpu_usage_percent` | 80 | 90 |
| `mem_used_percent` | 70 | 80 |
| `disk_used_percentage` | 80 | 90 |
| `load_15min` | 8 | 10 |

### How a threshold engages

Each metric in a profile has two levels, **Warning** and **Unhealthy**. Each level has a threshold and a period, from 1 minute to 24 hours.

A level engages when the metric's median over that level's period reaches the threshold. In practice, that means at least half the readings in the period are at or past the threshold. A single odd reading does not change a device's status.

By default a metric is unhealthy at or above its thresholds. You can flip a metric so that it is unhealthy at or below them, for values where low is the problem.

<Note>
  A device nobody is watching reports once an hour by default. A short period then holds one reading at most. Use a period of several hours if you want more than one reading to count.
</Note>

### Edit a profile

You need [a role that](/access/roles) can update the product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    Open the product, go to **Settings**, and select the **Device health** tab. Pick a profile under **Profiles**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or change metrics">
    Use **Add a metric…** to add any metric your devices report, or a built-in one. For each metric, set the **Warning at** and **Unhealthy at** thresholds and their periods. Click the `≥` or `≤` button to flip which direction is unhealthy.

    NervesHub offers **Use suggested** on a metric when it has at least a week of your fleet's readings for it and its suggestion differs from the thresholds you have. The suggestion marks what is unusual for your fleet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the featured metrics">
    Click the star in the **Featured** column. A featured metric gets a tile at the top of the device page. Metrics that are not featured still count toward health and still show on the **Health** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the effect and save">
    The bar above the metrics shows how many of the profile's devices are healthy, warning, unhealthy or without data **Right now**. While you have unsaved changes it shows the counts **If you save**. Click **Save profile**, or **Discard**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a platform profile

On the **Device health** tab, click **Add a platform profile** and choose a platform. The list holds the platforms reported by firmware uploaded to the product that do not have a profile yet.

To remove a platform profile, open it and click **Delete profile**. Its devices go back to **Default**. You cannot delete **Default**.

### Built-in metrics

A profile can include a metric that NervesHub measures itself:

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Disconnects** | How many times the device disconnected during the period. |

A built-in metric cannot be featured.

## Metric charts

The device's **Health** tab has a **History** card with a chart for each metric the device reports.

- Choose a time frame of **3 hours**, **1 day** or **7 days**.
- Drag across a chart to zoom all of them. Click **Reset zoom** to go back.
- Switch between **Line** and **Scatter** chart styles.

Sizes that only qualify another metric (`mem_size_mb`, `disk_total_kb`, `disk_available_kb`) are not charted.

Metric history is kept for 30 days. Each reading records the firmware the device was running, so you can compare a metric across releases.

You can also read history from outside NervesHub:

```bash
nh device metrics thermostat-4021 --key cpu_temp,mem_used_percent --since 2h
```

```text
GET /api/orgs/{org_name}/products/{product_name}/devices/{identifier}/metrics
GET /api/devices/{identifier}/metrics
```

See the [CLI reference](/cli/reference) and the [API overview](/api/overview).

## Alarms

Alarms come from the device. Your firmware raises and clears them, and NervesHub shows the set that is currently raised. NervesHub does not raise an alarm when a metric crosses a threshold: thresholds change the device's [health status](#health-status), not its alarms.

The **Alarms** row sits at the foot of the **Health** card on the **Details** tab and the **Now** card on the **Health** tab. It shows how many alarms are active. Each alarm is listed with its name, its description, and when it was raised. With no alarms it reads **None active**.

The row appears when Health or Alarms is turned on for both the product and the device.

How quickly an alarm shows up depends on how the device sends it:

| Sent through | NervesHub learns of a change |
| --- | --- |
| Health | At the next health report. That is hourly by default, or every minute while somebody has the device's page open. |
| Alarms | When the device raises or clears the alarm. |

A device that disconnects keeps its alarms in NervesHub. They are corrected when it reconnects and reports again.

To set up alarms in firmware, see [Alarms in the Health extension](/extensions/health#alarms).

## What works without ClickHouse

| Feature | Without ClickHouse |
| --- | --- |
| Current metric values | Shown. |
| Current alarms | Shown. |
| Health status | Worked out from the latest report alone, against the fixed [default thresholds](#default-thresholds). Your health profile is not used. |
| Metric history charts | Empty. The **Health** tab says metric history needs analytics. |
| Alarm history | Not recorded. |
| `GET .../metrics` | Answers `501`. |
