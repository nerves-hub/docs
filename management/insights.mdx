NervesHub shows the current state of every device, and the history of how that changed: when devices connected, how updates ended, and what messages crossed each connection.

## Requirements

* NervesHub 2.5.0 or later.
* ClickHouse on the NervesHub instance for everything historical. Without it, the parts marked below are hidden or empty. See [Feature prerequisites](/hosting/feature-prerequisites#clickhouse).
* The **Insights** page is closed to members whose custom role is limited to devices with certain tags. See [Roles](/access/roles).

| Where | What | Needs ClickHouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Insights** | What the fleet is made of, **Failing updates**, **Notifications** | No |
| **Insights** | Trend charts, **Flapping** | Yes |
| Device **Health** tab | Connection lane | Yes |
| Device **Firmware History** tab | **Installed firmware** | No |
| Device **Firmware History** tab | **Update attempts** | Yes |
| Device **Data History** tab | Messages sent and received | Yes |
| Deployment group **Stats** tab | Update outcomes and durations | Yes |
| Deployment group **Stats** tab | Download totals | No |
| Firmware page | Count of failed installs over the last 7 days | Yes |

Nothing has to be turned on per product or in firmware. Health figures on **Insights** depend on devices reporting health. See [Health monitoring](/management/health-monitoring).

## Product Insights

In your product, select **Insights**. The page refreshes every minute while **Auto refresh** is on.

### What the fleet is made of

Three bars count every device in the product once each:

| Bar | Groups |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection** | **Online**, **Offline under 7 d**, **Not seen 7–14 d**, **Not seen 14 d+**, **Never connected** |
| **Health** | Healthy, warning, unhealthy, and unknown. A device that has never reported its health counts as unknown. |
| **Firmware** | The firmware version each device last reported |

Switch between **Count** and **Percent**. Every count links to the devices list filtered to the devices it counts.

### Health and connection by firmware version

The table under the bars crosses health and connection with firmware version. It names the four versions with the most devices and folds the rest into one row.

The **Stands out** card beside it names a version whose devices are notably less healthy than the rest of the fleet. Health is judged on active devices only: those online or seen in the last 7 days. A version stands out when all of these hold:

* It has at least 10 active devices.
* At least 3 of them are not healthy.
* Its share of not-healthy devices is at least twice the fleet's.

### Trends

The trends row shows how the fleet moved over a period. Pick **24 h**, **14 d**, **4 w**, or **90 d**. Days are in your time zone.

| Chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Firmware adoption** | How many devices ran each firmware version over the period. Switch between **Stacked** and **Lines**. |
| **Updates** | Update attempts that finished, split into succeeded and failed. An update the device rescheduled is neither. |
| **Online** | The share of the fleet connected |
| **Healthy** | The share of the fleet healthy |

With **Releases** on, each chart marks when the newest version was released, if that falls in the period.

Shares are of today's fleet. A device added during the period is counted from the start, and a removed device is not counted.

### Lists

| List | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Failing updates** | Devices that keep failing to update, with how many attempts failed in a row and for how long |
| **Flapping** | Devices that connected more than 10 times in the last hour, or whose health moved into warning or unhealthy more than 5 times in the last hour |
| **Notifications** | The latest [product notifications](/management/notifications) |

Connections that ended as planned do not count toward flapping: a device going to sleep, a reboot, a reconnect someone asked for, a revoked key, or a server restart.

## Connection history

NervesHub records each connection a device makes: when it was established, when it closed, and why.

Open a device and select the **Health** tab. The connection lane at the top of the **History** card covers the same time frame as the metric charts. It shows:

* When the device was online.
* When it was offline for a minute or more, and the reason the connection before it ended.
* The network interface and address the device came back on.
* Reconnects, where the device opened a new connection less than a minute after the last one.

The metric charts mark the same offline periods. Change the time frame, or drag across a chart to zoom.

## Firmware update history

Open a device and select the **Firmware History** tab. It has two views.

**Installed firmware** lists each firmware the device reported, newest first, with its validation status.

**Update attempts** lists how each update sent to the device ended, with when, the outcome, detail, and what sent it: a [deployment group](/setup/deployments) or a manual request. An attempt ends one of six ways:

| Outcome | Meaning | Counts as a failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Succeeded | The device came back running the firmware it was sent | No |
| Rescheduled | The device asked for the update to come back later | No |
| Failed | The device reported an error while updating | Yes |
| Ignored | The device declined the update | Yes |
| Expired | The update stopped making progress and timed out | Yes |
| Abandoned | The device came back still running its old firmware, without saying why | Yes |

A failure adds to the device's count of consecutive failed updates. That count is kept outside ClickHouse, so the **Failing updates** list on **Insights** works on every instance.

An attempt that has just ended can take a moment to appear in **Update attempts**.

The same update history feeds two other places that need ClickHouse. A deployment group's **Stats** tab counts how each release's attempts ended and how long they took. See [Deployment groups](/setup/deployments#stats-tab). A firmware's page counts its failed installs over the last 7 days.

## Data History

The **Data History** tab on a device shows every message that crossed the device's connection, in both directions, newest first. Use it to answer whether a device was ever sent a message, and what it sent back.

Each row has the time, direction, channel, event name, and data. Selecting a row lays out its payload.

Filter by direction (**Both ways**, **From device**, **To device**) and by channel (**All channels**, **Device**, **Console**, **Extensions**). Filters are kept in the URL, so you can link someone to a view.

New messages stream in while the tab is open. Pause streaming to read. Resuming fetches what arrived while paused. The tab holds up to 1,000 messages.

What is kept of each message depends on its channel:

| Channel | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Device and extensions | The payload, redacted and capped in size |
| Console | The size only, never the contents |
| [Local shell extension](/extensions/local-shell) | The size only, never the contents |

Console and shell traffic is what someone typed and what came back, which can include credentials.

If the tab shows "Analytics aren't enabled for your platform.", the instance runs without ClickHouse.
