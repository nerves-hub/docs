With two-factor authentication on, signing in asks for a code from an authenticator app after your password or Google sign-in. Each user turns it on for their own account. An organization's admins can require it of every member.

Codes are time-based one-time passwords (TOTP): six digits that change every 30 seconds. Any authenticator app that shows six-digit codes works.

## Requirements

- NervesHub 2.5.4 or later.
- `SECRETS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` set on the instance. The key encrypts each user's authenticator secret. Without it, two-factor authentication is not offered and no organization can require it. See [Feature prerequisites](/hosting/feature-prerequisites#secrets-encryption-key).

<Warning>
  If you self-host, do not change `SECRETS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` once users have two-factor authentication on. Codes from their apps can no longer be checked. Those users can only sign in with a recovery code, and then have to set their app up again.
</Warning>

## Turn it on for your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account page">
    Open your account page. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start setup">
    Click **Set up two-factor authentication**. If you have not signed in within the last 10 minutes, NervesHub asks you to confirm your identity first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add NervesHub to your authenticator app">
    Scan the QR code with your app. If you cannot scan it, type the key shown beside it into the app by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a code">
    Type the six-digit code the app shows and click **Turn on**. You have 10 minutes from starting setup to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    NervesHub shows ten recovery codes. Use **Copy** or **Download** to keep them somewhere safe, such as a password manager, then click **I've saved them**. They are not shown again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning two-factor authentication on signs out your other browser sessions. NervesHub emails you when it is turned on or off, and when new recovery codes are made.

## Sign in with a code

After your password or Google sign-in, NervesHub asks for an **Authentication code**. Type the six-digit code from your app and click **Verify**.

- Each code works once.
- A code from the 30 seconds before or after the current one is accepted, for clocks that drift.
- You get five tries every ten minutes.

## Recovery codes

Each of the ten recovery codes signs you in once without your app. At the sign-in step, click **Use a recovery code instead** and type one in.

Your account's **Two-factor authentication** page shows how many codes you have left. To replace them, click **Manage** on your account page, enter a code from the app or a recovery code under **New recovery codes**, and click **Make new codes**. The old codes stop working.

## Turn it off

On your account's **Two-factor authentication** page, enter a code from the app or a recovery code under **Turn off two-factor authentication** and click **Turn off**.

If an organization you belong to requires two-factor authentication, you cannot reach that organization until you turn it back on. Your account and your other organizations work as before.

## Require it for an organization

You need the built-in **Admin** role, and two-factor authentication on for your own account. This stops you from locking yourself out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the organization's settings">
    Open your organization and select the **Settings** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the requirement on">
    Under **Two-factor authentication**, tick **Require two-factor authentication for every member**. The page lists the members who do not have it on yet and would be turned away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the requirement starts:

- NervesHub emails every other member. Members without two-factor authentication are asked to set it up.
- A member without it is sent to their **Two-factor authentication** page when they open the organization.
- Until they turn it on, the organization's API, remote consoles, local shells, device event streams and [Slack](/outbound/slack) actions refuse them.
- Pages and consoles they have open in the organization close.

The change is recorded in the organization's [audit log](/access/audit-log).

## Effect on the API and CLI

- **Password sign-in to the API is refused.** `POST /api/users/auth` answers `401` for an account with two-factor authentication on. That is the endpoint `nh user auth` uses, so it fails too. Sign in through the browser with `nh user login`, or use an access token. See [API authentication](/api/authentication).
- **Organization requirement.** API requests for an organization that requires two-factor authentication answer `403` for a member who does not have it on. The message names the organization and says to turn it on.

## Limits

As of NervesHub 2.5.4 there is no admin reset. Keep your recovery codes: they are the only way in without your authenticator app.
